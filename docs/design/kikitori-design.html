01 Onboarding: welcome, audio permission

1 · Welcome
First launch. Window 640 × 520, not resizable, centred. Esc does nothing; ⌘Q quits.
聞
Welcome to kikitori
Live English subtitles for Japanese streams, with the context behind the jokes.
Hears the stream
Listens to one app, like Safari or Chrome. No extra audio drivers.
Transcribes on your Mac
Japanese speech becomes text on-device. Audio never leaves your Mac.
Translates with Claude
Subtitles arrive 2–5 seconds behind. Click any line to learn the slang, meme or reference.
2 · Audio access: not yet asked
“Allow Audio Access” triggers the macOS prompt for Screen & System Audio Recording.
Let kikitori hear your apps
kikitori listens to the sound of one app you choose. macOS calls this permission Screen & System Audio Recording. kikitori records audio only, never your screen.
Audio is turned into text on this Mac and then discarded. Nothing is recorded to disk unless you export a transcript.
3 · Audio access: granted
Shown when a test process tap opens without error. Advances automatically after 1.5 s, or on Continue.
Audio access is on
You can turn it off any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.
4 · Audio access: denied
Prompt dismissed or switch off. kikitori polls every 2 s while this screen is open and advances when access appears.
kikitori can’t hear your apps yet
Audio access is turned off. Turn it on in System Settings, then come back here.
  1. 1Click Open System Settings.
  2. 2Under Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on kikitori.
  3. 3If macOS asks, choose Quit & Reopen. kikitori comes back to this step.
